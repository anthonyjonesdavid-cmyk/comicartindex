About & methodology

Comic Art Index is a free price guide for original comic art from the "prime era," roughly 1985 to 1995: the years of Frank Miller's Dark Knight, Todd McFarlane's Spider-Man, Jim Lee's X-Men and the early Image boom.

What counts

  • Original art (covers, interior pages, splashes, spreads) created between 1985 and 1995.
  • Sales that were publicly reported, with the price, the venue, and a date.
  • Every row links to the page we checked when adding it. No estimated, asking, or invented prices.

How prices are reported

  • Auction prices are shown as reported by the source. Heritage and ComicConnect results generally include the buyer's premium.
  • Rows from Heritage auction #7470 (Sept 25–27, 2026) are dated to the auction's closing day; the exact session day for each lot may differ by a day or two.
  • The Uncanny X-Men #268 cover sale is dated to the CBR report (Dec 4, 2020).
  • The Dark Knight Returns Joker spread is dated to Heritage's Oct 6–8, 2023 event.

Images

We show only small, credited thumbnails, each credited to and linked to its auction listing at Heritage Auctions (HA.com) or Hake's. All artwork is © its creators and publishers.
